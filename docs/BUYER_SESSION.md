# Buyer-only virtual home-bar session

Eric selected a $19, 20-minute virtual session for purchasers of the existing $9 ebook. The request code is BARBUYER. Email intake is theboydsbusinessgroup@gmail.com.

Buyers may propose any future date and time with their timezone, including evenings and weekends. Every exact slot requires Eric's explicit approval before payment or booking. A request does not reserve a slot. Changing the date, time, timezone or duration requires fresh approval. This is open request intake, not a promise to host at every hour.

The buyer privately emails their ebook order ID and purchase email, timezone, preferred dates/times and one question. The operator verifies the qualifying purchase, resolves the exact 20-minute slot and checks Google Calendar for conflicts. Eric's connected calendar uses America/Chicago. A clear calendar cannot substitute for his approval; a missing or failed availability read leaves the request pending. Approval evidence must come from Eric, never from a buyer-supplied field.

After Eric approves the exact slot, confirm buyer acceptance and cancellation/rescheduling terms, then request the full $19 USD session payment. Verify a successful non-test payment with the provider, matched to this buyer and request for the full amount and currency. A screenshot, payment authorization or pending/failed/refunded/disputed payment is insufficient. No appointment, booking confirmation, meeting invitation or service is provided without verified full payment. Recheck calendar conflicts before creating the appointment and sending meeting details privately. No payment request, calendar hold, invitation or booking confirmation occurs before approval. One completed session per verified qualifying purchase. Code alone does not prove purchase or apply a payment discount.

Paid buyers are owed the agreed session. If Eric cannot provide it, the buyer chooses a replacement slot with fresh Eric approval or a full refund of the $19 session fee. A calendar conflict or event-creation error after payment leaves the request PAID_AWAITING_RESOLUTION, not BOOKED; resolve it with the buyer rather than leaving it pending indefinitely. Rescheduling does not incur another session charge. Do not describe a refund as completed until the provider confirms it. Buyer cancellation/no-show terms must be disclosed and accepted before payment.

Request intake is advertised through the YouTube channel and two existing offer-video descriptions. Future videos use the matching offer and spoken CTA. Purchase verification, approval, scheduling and payment arrangement remain manual; no automatic calendar reservation, public booking page or paid checkout is deployed. Receipts, buyer emails, approval records and meeting links stay private. For adults of legal drinking age; drinking is not required.

The private Boyd's Bar OS files config/offers/ebook-buyer-session.json and docs/offers/EBOOK_BUYER_SESSION.md contain operator rules and prepared receipt copy.
