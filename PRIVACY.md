# Media Engine Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** September 11, 2026

Media Engine is an internal content management and publishing system operated by Boyd's Business Group. It is used to create, manage, publish, and measure original content on accounts owned or authorized by the operator.

## Information we access

When a platform account is connected, Media Engine may access information made available through the platform's authorized API, such as account identifiers, boards or publishing destinations, posts created by the application, engagement metrics, and other data required to publish and measure content.

Media Engine does not request or intentionally collect passwords through the application. Authentication should occur through the platform's supported OAuth or API authorization process.

## How information is used

Information is used only to operate the content workflow, including creating and publishing authorized content, avoiding duplicate publication, monitoring delivery status, measuring content performance, troubleshooting failures, and improving future content decisions.

## Sharing and sale of information

Media Engine does not sell personal information. Data is not shared with third parties except service providers or platform APIs necessary to operate the system, or when required by law.

## Data retention and security

Only information reasonably necessary to operate and audit the system is retained. Credentials and access tokens are intended to be stored in protected secret-management systems and are not committed to public source code. Reasonable technical safeguards are used to limit access to authorized systems and operators.

## User choices and deletion

A connected platform account may be disconnected or its authorization revoked through the relevant platform. Requests concerning deletion of data held specifically by Media Engine may be submitted using the contact information below.

## Third-party platforms

Use of connected platforms is also subject to those platforms' own terms and privacy policies. Media Engine does not control the privacy practices of third-party platforms.

## Changes

This policy may be updated as Media Engine adds or changes integrations. The effective date above will be revised when material changes are made.
