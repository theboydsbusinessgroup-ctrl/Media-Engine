What we collect

When you sign up we collect the email address you enter. Our email provider also records the date and time of your signup, your IP address, and whether you open or click the emails we send.

We use your email address to send the free Bottle 1 sample, practical cocktail tips, and occasional offers for our own products, such as the full One Bottle at a Time guide. We do not sell or rent your email address.

Who processes it

Emails are sent through MailerLite, which stores the subscriber list on our behalf. The sample PDF is hosted on Google Drive, and the full guide is sold through Gumroad, which handles its own checkout and payment data. This page loads fonts from Google Fonts. It does not use advertising or analytics trackers.

Your choices

Every email has an unsubscribe link, and unsubscribing takes effect right away. To have your details deleted, or to ask what we hold about you, email theboydsbusinessgroup@gmail.com.
